T.E.S.S. Information: Levels

There are 6 main levels in T.E.S.S., being the initial 4 levels in The End, and the 2 levels in the Aether. You must kill the bosses in The End to move to Levels 2, 3, and 4. You must kill 500 enemies in The End to enter The Aether. To reach Level 6, you must kill the Queen after killing a total of 700 enemies. Level 6 is the highest possible level, and going past this will be endless enemies.
