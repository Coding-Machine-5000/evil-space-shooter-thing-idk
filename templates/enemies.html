T.E.S.S. Information: Enemies

ENEMY: Enderman Ship

Enderman Ships are the regular enemy in The End, and give 100 points on kill. These enemies are worth 1 towards the escape counter.

BOSS: King of the End

The King is the first boss you will fight in the End, and will be fought twice; once at 100 enemies killed and again at 200 enemies killed. All bosses that escape instantly end your game.

BOSS: Emperor of the End

The Emperor is the second boss you will fight in the End, and is only fought once at 300 enemies killed. The Emperor is much stronger, but you will begin to fire faster once you begin the fight.

ENEMY: Valkyrie Ship

Valkyries only show up once you enter The Aether at 500 enemies killed. They give 200 points on death. Other than their score value, Valkyries and Endermen are functionally identical.

BOSS: Valkyrie Queen

The Queen is the final boss that you fight, ending in the Aether. She shows up at 700 enemies killed, and is more powerful than the Emperor of the End. She gives 25000 score on death.
