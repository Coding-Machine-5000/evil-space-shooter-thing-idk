About T.E.S.S.

The End Space Shooter, also known as T.E.S.S., is a shoot em' up style space shooter game themed around the Minecraft End Dimension. You play as an armed ship named the S.S. Steve, fighting against enemy Enderman fleets. At a certain point, you will fight the King of the End, and then the Emperor of the End, eventually traveling to the most famous Minecraft modded dimension: The Aether, where you will now fight enemy Valkyrie ships, and eventually the Valkyrie Queen ship.
